User Documentation for "Fun Attendance Calculator"

Overview

This Attendance Calculator helps students figure out how many lecturer they can bunk while maintaining their required attendance percentage, or how many more lecturer they need to attend to reach the desired percentage. You can select different attendance goals, such as 60%, 65%, 70%, 75%, and more.

How to Use

  • Enter your current number of lecturer attended in the "lecturer Present" field.
  • Enter the total number of academic lecturer in the "Total lecturer" field.
  • Select your required attendance percentage from the dropdown menu.
  • Click the "Calculate" button to see the results.
  • The calculator will tell you how many lecturer you can still bunk or how many more lecturer you need to attend to meet the required percentage.
  • View the graphical representation of your attendance before and after in the chart below.
