User Documentation for "Attendance Calculator"

Overview

The Attendance Calculator helps students figure out how many days they can bunk while maintaining their required attendance percentage, or how many more days they need to attend to reach the desired percentage. You can select different attendance goals, such as 60%, 65%, 70%, 75%, and more.

How to Use

  • Enter your current number of days attended in the "Days Present" field.
  • Enter the total number of academic days in the "Total Days" field.
  • Select your required attendance percentage from the dropdown menu.
  • Click the "Calculate" button to see the results.
  • The calculator will tell you how many days you can still bunk or how many more days you need to attend to meet the required percentage.
