Course Load Planner

Detailed Documentation

The Course Load Planner is a tool designed to assist students in managing their course load for each semester. It helps in balancing the workload while ensuring that graduation requirements are met efficiently.

  • Course Name: Enter the name of the course.
  • Credits: Enter the number of credits for the course.
  • Semester: Enter the semester number for which the course is planned.
  • Target GPA: Enter the target GPA for the semester.
  • Add Course: Click the 'Add Course' button to add the course to the plan. The table below will update to reflect the planned courses.
  • Total Credits: The total number of credits planned for the semester is displayed.
  • Balance Workload: Adjust the number of courses or credits to balance the workload across semesters.

The planner can be used iteratively to adjust the course load until a balanced and manageable schedule is achieved, ensuring a steady progress towards graduation.
