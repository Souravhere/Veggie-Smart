Documentation

Overview

This Course Load Planner is designed to help students manage their course load each semester by allowing them to add courses, their credit hours, and their difficulty level. The tool calculates the total workload and provides a visual representation of the course distribution.

Features

  • Add multiple courses with their name, credit hours, and difficulty level.
  • Calculate the total course load based on the inputs provided.
  • Visualize the course load distribution using a pie chart.
  • Ensure a balanced workload to avoid overloading yourself in a single semester.

Example

Let's say a student is planning their semester and wants to ensure they don't overload themselves. They have the following courses:

  • Mathematics: 4 credits, Difficulty 3
  • Physics: 3 credits, Difficulty 4
  • Chemistry: 4 credits, Difficulty 2
  • Computer Science: 3 credits, Difficulty 5

Using this tool, they can enter each course with its corresponding details, calculate the total load, and view a pie chart showing the percentage distribution of each course. This helps them understand their workload and make informed decisions about their course selection.

Formulas

  • Total Load: Sum of all credit hours.
  • Course Distribution: (Credits of each course / Total Credits) * 100 to get the percentage distribution.

Usage

1. Enter the course name, credit hours, and difficulty level for each course.

2. Click "Add Course" to add the course to the list.

3. Once all courses are added, click "Calculate Course Load" to view the total load and chart.

4. The chart will display the distribution of course load, helping you to balance your semester efficiently.
