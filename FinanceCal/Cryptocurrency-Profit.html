Cryptocurrency Profit Calculator

How the Cryptocurrency Profit Calculator Works

The Cryptocurrency Profit Calculator is designed to help you calculate the potential profit or loss from your cryptocurrency investments.

  • Initial Investment: This is the amount of money you initially invested in purchasing the cryptocurrency.
  • Buy Price per Coin: The price at which you bought each cryptocurrency coin.
  • Sell Price per Coin: The price at which you plan to sell or have sold each cryptocurrency coin.
  • Number of Coins: The total number of cryptocurrency coins you own or plan to sell.

The calculator uses the following formula to calculate your profit or loss:

Profit/Loss = (Sell Price per Coin - Buy Price per Coin) * Number of Coins

The result will tell you whether you have made a profit or loss based on the difference between your buy price and sell price, multiplied by the number of coins.
